# Photo-to-Clean-PDF
Photo to Clean PDF (photo-to-clean-pdf)An AI agent skill that transforms raw camera photos of physical documents, exam sheets, and worksheets into clean, flat, shadow-free, print-ready PDF files — either as a restored document scan or a freshly typeset vector PDF complete with extracted diagrams and logos.Key FeaturesPerspective Correction & Page Flattening: Converts tilted, curled, or angled tabletop photos of paper sheets into flat, top-down digital document pages on a pure white background.Shadow & Handwritten Mark Removal: Eliminates harsh lighting gradients, phone shadows, paper creases, and handwritten annotations (such as crosses X, checkmarks, grading scores, and student notes) while cleanly restoring the underlying printed question numbers and text.Dual PDF Export Modes:Cleaned Scan PDF Mode: Combines the restored, shadow-free document images sequentially into a multi-page PDF ready for printing.Retyped Vector PDF Mode: Crops and cleans embedded diagrams and school/organization logos, transcribes all printed questions and headers into crisp vector typography, fixes obvious spelling typos from the original print, and compiles a publication-grade PDF.Mode ComparisonModeBest Used ForOutput CharacteristicsCleaned Scan PDFFast cleanup of photographed worksheets or examsPreserves exact original visual layout on a clean white backgroundRetyped Vector PDFCreating a fresh master copy of an exam or handoutRazor-sharp vector fonts, corrected typos, and extracted high-contrast diagramsRepository Structurephoto-to-clean-pdf/

InstallationOption 1: Install via CLI / Skill RegistryIf your environment supports installing skills directly from a GitHub repository:npx skills add <your-github-username>/photo-to-clean-pdf
Option 2: Manual Upload via Skills ManagerDownload SKILL.md from this repository (or download the full repository as a .zip archive).Open the Skills Manager in your AI workspace and upload SKILL.md (or the .zip file).Alternatively, attach SKILL.md directly in a chat session and prompt:"Please save this SKILL.md file as a new skill."Example PromptsAttach one or more photos of your document or exam pages and use prompts such as:Clean and export directly to PDF (English):"Clean and straighten these 3 exam page photos into flat white document pages, remove all shadows and handwritten cross marks, and export them as a PDF."Retype all questions and diagrams into a vector PDF (English):"Retype all the questions from these photos neatly into a new PDF, including the header logo and all biological diagrams."Clean and export to PDF (Indonesian):"Tolong rapikan 3 foto lembar soal di atas menjadi kertas putih bersih tanpa bayangan, hilangkan semua tanda silang dan coretan, lalu ekspor ke PDF."How It WorksVisual Inspection: Analyzes uploaded photos to detect page order, numbering sequence, diagrams, and handwritten markings.Document Restoration: Generates flat, top-down, pure-white document pages free of shadows and pen/pencil marks, verifying that sequential numbering remains intact.Asset Extraction & Typesetting: Crops diagrams using verified coordinate grids, normalizes background pixels to pure white (#FFFFFF), and compiles either a direct multi-page scan PDF or a headless Chromium vector PDF.Quality Verification: Renders page previews to inspect vertical balance, margin cleanliness, and diagram legibility before delivering the final PDF.LicenseDistributed under the MIT License.
